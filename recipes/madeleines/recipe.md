# Madeleines

## Ingredients

- 40 g whole milk
- 20 g liquid honey
- 130 g unsalted butter
- 100 g whole egg, approximately 2 large eggs without shells
- 85 g sugar
- 130 g flour
- 6 g baking powder

## Steps

1. Heat the milk and honey together to 60°C.
2. In a separate saucepan, melt the butter and heat it to 60°C, until it begins to foam.
3. Whip the eggs and sugar with a whisk at medium speed for approximately 15 minutes, until thick and at ribbon stage.
4. Reduce the mixer to low speed and gradually stream the warm milk and honey into the aerated egg mixture.
5. Sift the flour and baking powder together, then mix them into the batter just until incorporated.
6. With the mixer running gently, gradually stream in the warm melted butter and mix until fully incorporated.
7. Cover the batter and refrigerate for at least 2 hours; an overnight rest is preferable.
8. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Divide the cold batter among 18 cavities in a metal madeleine pan, using two spoons or a piping bag with a large plain tip.
9. Bake for approximately 10–12 minutes, until well browned with distinct, slightly paler humps.

## Notes

- This formulation is planned and has not yet been tested by the user.
- The characteristic hump depends on thermal shock: thoroughly chilled batter must go directly into a fully preheated oven.
- Use a metal madeleine pan for effective heat transfer.
- The total batter weight is approximately 510 g, or roughly 28 g per madeleine, subject to the capacity of the pan.
