# Financiers

## Ingredients

- 150 g unsalted butter, to yield approximately 125 g strained browned butter
- 100 g finely ground almond flour
- 160 g icing sugar
- 50 g pastry flour
- 2 g fine sea salt
- 150 g egg whites
- 15 g mild honey
- 2 g vanilla extract
- 10 g softened butter, for the molds

## Steps

1. Brown the 150 g butter to a deep hazelnut color, approximately 155–160°C. Strain and cool it to 45–50°C; the expected yield is approximately 125 g.
2. Whisk together the almond flour, icing sugar, pastry flour, and salt.
3. Add the egg whites, honey, and vanilla. Mix only until smooth, without aerating the batter.
4. Stream in the warm browned butter and mix until thoroughly emulsified.
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
6. Butter the financier molds and portion approximately 50 g batter into each cavity, filling each approximately 80% full.
7. Bake at 200°C conventional for 5 minutes, then reduce the oven to 180°C and bake for another 8–11 minutes.
8. Remove the financiers when deeply browned at the edges, domed, and springy in the center. Rest in the molds for 2 minutes, then unmold onto a rack.

## Notes

- This formulation is planned and has not yet been tested by the user.
- The approximately 600 g batch makes 12 large financiers at approximately 50 g each, or 14–15 smaller financiers at 40–45 g each.
- For a pistachio test using 100% unsweetened pistachio paste, replace 40 g of the almond flour with 40 g pistachio paste; this variation remains untested and is recorded in the log.
- The best same-day texture is a crisp, caramelized exterior surrounding a moist center.
