# Recipe Log

## 2026-10-02 — Proposed dinner for six

- Status: planned, not reported as cooked.
- Change: selected for the miso cod dinner menu; initial quantities and method proposed by the assistant.
- Quantities: 240 g whole oat groats; 1,800 g water; 8 g fine salt; 250 g cucumber; 150 g radishes; 40 g scallions; 35 g parsley; 15 g mint leaves; 40 g lemon juice; 15 g unseasoned rice vinegar; 35 g olive oil; 10 g sesame seeds.
- Timing: active 20 min; total 65–90 min including cooling; finish 5 min.
- Method — Cook the groats · 35–50 min: Rinse the groats. Bring the water and 6 g of the salt to a boil, add the oats, and simmer gently until tender with a pleasantly chewy centre. Begin checking at 30 minutes; whole groats may need up to 60 minutes. Add boiling water if needed.
- Method — Drain and cool · 20–30 min: Drain thoroughly, spread thinly on a shallow tray, and refrigerate promptly to cool. Do not leave in a deep warm pile. Keep chilled until assembly.
- Method — Prepare vegetables and dressing · 10 min: Dice the cucumber; thinly slice the radishes and scallions. Chop the parsley and mint. Whisk lemon juice, rice vinegar, olive oil and the remaining 2 g salt.
- Method — Assemble · 5 min: Toss the cooled groats with the dressing. Fold in the vegetables and herbs near serving. Finish with toasted sesame seeds.
- Make ahead: Cook the groats and mix the dressing up to 24 hours ahead. Store separately in the refrigerator. Keep chopped vegetables separate; add herbs and sesame at service.
