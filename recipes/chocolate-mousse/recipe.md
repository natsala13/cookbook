# Chocolate Mousse

## Ingredients

- 150 g 70% dark chocolate
- 4 large eggs, separated; approximately 200 g without shells

## Steps

1. Melt the chocolate gently and cool it to approximately 35–40°C.
2. Whisk the egg yolks into the melted chocolate until smooth.
3. Whip the egg whites to soft, glossy peaks.
4. Fold one-third of the whites into the chocolate mixture to loosen it, then fold in the remainder.
5. Portion and refrigerate for at least 3 hours, until set.

## Notes

- No sugar or cream is added; the sweetness and texture come entirely from the chocolate and eggs.
- Use very fresh eggs and a well-tempered chocolate mixture so the mousse remains light rather than grainy.

## Last Log

- Simplified to 150 g 70% chocolate and 4 eggs, with no sugar or cream.
