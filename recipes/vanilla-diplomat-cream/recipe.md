# Vanilla Diplomat Cream

## Ingredients

- 400 g whole milk
- 4 g vanilla bean, split and scraped
- 80 g egg yolks
- 72 g granulated sugar
- 32 g cornstarch
- 1 g fine salt
- 4 g powdered gelatin, 200 Bloom
- 24 g cold water, for blooming the gelatin
- 32 g unsalted butter
- 240 g heavy cream, 35% fat, cold

## Steps

1. Bloom the gelatin in the cold water for 10 minutes.
2. Heat the milk with the vanilla seeds and pod to approximately 85°C. Cover, infuse for 15 minutes, then remove the pod and restore any evaporated milk so the mixture weighs 400 g.
3. Whisk the yolks, sugar, cornstarch, and salt until completely smooth. Temper with the hot milk, return everything to the saucepan, and bring to a full boil while whisking continuously.
4. Cook at a boil for 60 seconds, whisking continuously. Remove from the heat, add the bloomed gelatin and butter, and blend until fully emulsified.
5. Transfer to a shallow container, cover directly on the surface, and cool promptly over an ice bath. Refrigerate overnight once it is no longer hot.
6. The next day, gently warm the pastry cream over a water bath while whisking until smooth, pliable, and approximately 25–28°C; do not heat it beyond what is needed to soften the gelatin.
7. Whip the cold heavy cream to soft-medium peaks. Fold one-third into the pastry cream to loosen it, then gently fold in the remainder.
8. Fill the cake immediately, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours before unmoulding or slicing.

## Notes

- The first test is in progress, using an overnight rest for the pastry-cream base before adding the whipped cream.
- The 4 g of 200-Bloom gelatin produces a clean slice while keeping the cream supple. For a soft filling contained by a tart shell or cake ring, 3 g may be sufficient; this has not yet been tested.
- The finished yield is approximately 850 g, allowing for minor cooking loss.
