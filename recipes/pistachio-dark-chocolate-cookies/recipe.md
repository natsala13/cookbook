# Pistachio Dark Chocolate Cookies

## Ingredients

- 115 g unsalted butter, softened to 18–20°C
- 120 g brown sugar
- 40 g white sugar
- 50 g whole egg
- 5 g vanilla extract
- 190 g flour
- 120 g roasted pistachios total, divided as follows:
  - 40 g finely ground
  - 80 g roughly chopped
- 2 g baking powder
- 1.5 g baking soda
- 3 g fine salt
- 100 g 60–65% dark chocolate, roughly chopped
- Flaky salt, optional

## Steps

1. Cream the softened butter, brown sugar, and white sugar with a paddle at medium-high speed for 3–4 minutes, until aerated and slightly lighter.
2. Add the egg and vanilla, then mix until fully emulsified, scraping down the bowl as needed.
3. Combine the flour, ground pistachios, baking powder, baking soda, and fine salt separately.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture and mix only until no dry flour remains. Fold in the chopped pistachios and dark chocolate.
5. Portion into 12 tall 60–65 g domes. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours.
6. Bake on a paper-lined tray at 175°C conventional heat for 11–13 minutes, until the edges are set and lightly golden but the centers remain soft.
7. Finish with flaky salt if desired. Rest on the tray for 10 minutes before moving.

## Notes

- This formulation is planned and has not yet been tested.
- Use 60–65% dark chocolate so it does not overwhelm the pistachio.
- Lightly roasting the pistachios intensifies their flavor; cool them completely before grinding or mixing into the dough.
- Orange-blossom water is deliberately omitted. For a future variation, test either 1–2 g orange-blossom water, 1–2 g finely grated orange zest, or 0.5 g ground cardamom—not all three at once.

- The first batch was baked. Leave the cookies on the oven tray for 10 minutes to set, then transfer them to a wire rack; final evaluation is pending.
