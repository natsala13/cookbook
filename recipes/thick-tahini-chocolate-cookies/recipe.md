# Thick Tahini Chocolate Cookies

## Ingredients

- 100 g unsalted butter
- 130 g tahini (approximately 120 ml)
- 200 g sugar
- 50 g whole egg
- 18 g egg yolk
- 5 g vanilla extract
- 165 g flour
- 2.5 g baking soda
- 2 g baking powder
- 3 g fine salt
- 100–120 g dark chocolate, chopped
- Flaky salt, optional

## Steps

1. Melt the butter and let it cool for approximately 5 minutes, until warm rather than hot.
2. Whisk the butter, tahini, and sugar together. Add the whole egg, yolk, and vanilla, then whisk until smooth.
3. Combine the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and fine salt separately. Add them to the wet mixture and mix just until no dry flour remains. Fold in the chocolate.
4. Portion the dough into tall 50–55 g cylinders or domes; do not flatten them.
5. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour, preferably 2 hours. Alternatively, freeze for 25–30 minutes.
6. Bake on a paper-lined tray at 180°C conventional heat for 10–12 minutes, with ample space between the cookies. Remove when the edges are golden and set but the centers remain visibly soft.
7. Sprinkle with flaky salt if desired and leave on the tray for 5–10 minutes before moving.

## Notes

- The listed dough weight makes approximately 14 cookies at 50–55 g each; make larger portions if producing only 10–12 cookies.
- Judge doneness by the set, golden edges rather than the center. The nearly underbaked center firms as it cools.
- Chill the shaped dough thoroughly to limit spread.
