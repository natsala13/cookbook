# Recipe Log

## 2026-10-02 — Proposed dinner for six

- Status: planned, not reported as cooked.
- Change: selected for the miso cod dinner menu; initial quantities and method proposed by the assistant.
- Quantities: 500 g raw peeled, deveined shrimp; 400 g crushed tomatoes; 25 g olive oil; 15 g garlic; 0.5 g chili flakes; 3 g fine salt; 10 g parsley; 2 g lemon zest; 10 g lemon juice.
- Timing: active 15 min; total 30–35 min; finish 10–12 min with sauce made ahead.
- Method — Build the base · 4 min: Warm olive oil in a wide sauté pan. Add thinly sliced garlic and cook gently for 1–2 minutes without browning. Add chili flakes, then crushed tomatoes and salt.
- Method — Reduce · 15–20 min: Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until thick enough to coat the shrimp. Keep the sauce savoury; add no sugar. Stop here for make-ahead preparation.
- Method — Cook the shrimp · 4–6 min: If the sauce was chilled, reheat it thoroughly to 74°C, about 5–7 minutes. Add dry, fully thawed shrimp in a single layer and turn as needed. Cook until opaque and pearly throughout, aiming for 63°C in the thickest part; timing depends on size.
- Method — Finish · 1 min: Fold in chopped parsley, lemon zest and lemon juice. Transfer to a shallow warm platter and serve immediately.
- Make ahead: Reduce the tomato sauce up to 24 hours ahead, cool promptly in a shallow container and refrigerate. Keep raw shrimp separately refrigerated; cook at service.
