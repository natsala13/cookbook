## 2026-09-27 — Proposed whole-grain spelt substitution

- User comment: "out of flour.... can I use whole wheat spelt instead?"
- Proposed change: replace 200 g all-purpose flour with 200 g whole-grain spelt flour and add 15 g water to the wet mixture. This is an untested starting point for this formula, not a performed substitution.
- Rationale: [King Arthur's whole-grain guide](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/learn/guides/whole-grains) advises gentle handling of spelt and extra liquid for fully whole-wheat cookie dough that chills before scooping. The 15 g water suggestion extrapolates that guidance to this spelt batch; absorption varies by flour.
- Method: mix only until incorporated; chill at least 2–3 hours, preferably overnight. Bake two 12 g test cookies at 165°C conventional heat, checking at 10 minutes (baseline range 10–12 minutes); assess spread and cooled texture before baking the rest. Keep other ingredients unchanged.
- Expected differences: nuttier flavor and potentially more grainy or crumbly texture. No guarantee of identical spread or crinkles.
- Result: none reported; recipe page remains the selected all-purpose baseline.
- Next test: confirm actual substitution and water amount used, then record cooled texture, spread and baking time.
