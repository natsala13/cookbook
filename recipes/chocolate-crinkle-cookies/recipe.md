# Chocolate Crinkle Cookies

## Ingredients

- 227 g bittersweet chocolate
- 113 g unsalted butter
- 135 g granulated sugar
- 150 g whole egg, without shells
- 9 g vanilla extract
- 200 g all-purpose flour
- 2 g baking powder
- 1.5 g fine salt
- 4 g instant espresso powder, optional
- 115 g icing sugar for coating; some will remain unused

## Steps

1. Melt chocolate with butter; stir smooth.
2. Beat eggs, granulated sugar, vanilla and optional espresso together. Incorporate chocolate mixture, baking powder and salt, then flour.
3. Cover and refrigerate 2–3 hours or overnight.
4. Shape small balls, coat in icing sugar and space approximately 4 cm apart on parchment-lined trays.
5. Bake at 163°C for 10–12 minutes, rotating trays midway. Cool on a rack.
