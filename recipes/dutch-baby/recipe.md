# Dutch Baby

## Ingredients

- 180 g eggs, without shells (about 3 Israeli size-L eggs; weigh after shelling)
- 185 g whole milk (approximately 180 ml)
- 95 g plain flour
- 25 g sugar
- 2 g fine salt
- 2 g vanilla extract
- 45 g unsalted butter
- 15 g lemon juice, for serving (optional)
- 10 g icing sugar, for serving (optional)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 230°C conventional with a 25 cm cast-iron skillet on the middle rack for at least 20 minutes.
2. Blend the eggs, milk, flour, sugar, salt, and vanilla until completely smooth, about 20 seconds. Rest the batter for 15 minutes, then briefly blend again.
3. Carefully remove the hot skillet, add the butter, and swirl until melted and lightly foaming, coating the base and sides.
4. Immediately pour in the batter and return the skillet to the oven.
5. Bake without opening the door until deeply golden, dramatically risen, and set in the centre, approximately 18–22 minutes.
6. Serve immediately, optionally finished with the lemon juice and icing sugar.

## Notes

- This formula produces more batter and is more flour-forward than the initial test formula, giving a thicker, more structured centre.
- Use room-temperature eggs and milk to maximize the initial rise.
- The target pan size is 25 cm; the bake time is provisional because no result has yet been recorded for this larger formula.
