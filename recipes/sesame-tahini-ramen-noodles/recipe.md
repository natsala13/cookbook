# Sesame-Tahini Ramen Noodles

## Ingredients

- 25 g soy sauce
- 15 g tahini or peanut butter
- 10 g rice vinegar
- 8 g sesame oil
- 6 g sugar or honey
- 4 g garlic, finely grated
- 10 g chili crisp, or to taste
- 1 portion fresh ramen noodles
- 30–50 g noodle water, as needed

## Steps

1. Stir together the soy sauce, tahini or peanut butter, rice vinegar, sesame oil, sugar or honey, garlic, and chili crisp.
2. Cook the fresh ramen noodles and reserve 30–50 g of the noodle water.
3. Toss the hot noodles with the sauce, adding noodle water as needed until glossy and loosened.

## Notes

- Finish with sliced scallions, sesame seeds, or a soft-boiled egg if available.
- The sauce is intentionally quick and flexible; adjust chili crisp to taste.
