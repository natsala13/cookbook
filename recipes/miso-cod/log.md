## 2026-10-02 — Proposed dinner for six

- Status: planned, not reported as cooked.
- Change: selected for the miso cod dinner menu; initial quantities and method proposed by the assistant.
- Quantities: 1,200 g boneless cod loin; 75 g white miso; 25 g sugar; 20 g unseasoned rice vinegar; 10 g soy sauce; 25 g water; 12 g ginger; 10 g neutral oil; 15 g scallions.
- Timing: active 15 min; total 45–60 min; finish 15–20 min.
- Method — Mix the glaze · 5 min: Whisk white miso, sugar, rice vinegar, soy sauce, water and finely grated ginger until smooth. This is a quick, sake- and mirin-free glaze.
- Method — Coat and chill · 5 min + 20–30 min resting: Pat the cod dry. Coat lightly with the glaze and refrigerate for 20–30 minutes. Make the glaze ahead, but coat the cod shortly before cooking; no overnight cure is needed.
- Method — Roast together · 12–18 min: Heat the oven to 220°C conventional / 200°C fan. Lightly oil a foil-lined tray. Arrange the six portions with space between them; leave excess glaze behind. Roast on the upper-middle rack, checking at 10 minutes. Thickness determines the time.
- Method — Finish and serve · 2 min: If needed, briefly grill/broil for 1–2 minutes for colour, watching constantly. Cook the thickest centre to 63°C; shield the glaze with foil if it darkens too early. Discard raw-fish-contact glaze. Scatter sliced scallions over the platter.
- Make ahead: Glaze: up to 24 hours ahead, covered and refrigerated. Portion and pin-bone the cod ahead; keep it separate and cold until coating.
